Homeowner & Property Access Consent

Property Entry & Pre-Arrival Provisioning Authorization Agreement

Executed in favor of Craft Clean Services, LLC d/b/a Panhandle Provision (a Florida Limited Liability Company).

Terms of Authorization & Consent

1. Grant of Property Access & Scope of Services: The undersigned Property Owner or Authorized Representative ("Owner") hereby grants Craft Clean Services, LLC d/b/a Panhandle Provision ("Provider"), including its employees, officers, and vetted delivery personnel, express permission to enter the residential property identified below solely for the purpose of delivering, unpacking, and stocking pre-ordered groceries, beverages, and household provisions inside the kitchen, refrigerator, freezer, and pantry prior to Owner's arrival (typically between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM on the scheduled arrival date).
2. Representation of Ownership & Authority: Owner warrants and represents that they are the legal owner of the subject property (or a duly authorized agent acting with the legal owner's express permission) and possess full legal authority to grant temporary entry and supply door, lockbox, or gate access credentials to Provider.
3. Security, Access Credentials & Photo Verification: Provider agrees to treat all keypad codes, lockbox combinations, and entry instructions as strictly confidential and to use them exclusively to fulfill scheduled provisioning orders. Upon completing in-home stocking, Provider will capture time-stamped verification photographs of the stocked kitchen/refrigerator and ensure all exterior doors and entry points are securely locked upon departure.
4. Compliance with Florida Alcohol Laws: In strict compliance with Florida law, Provider does not deliver, place, or leave alcoholic beverages unattended inside an unoccupied residence. Only non-alcoholic groceries, beverages, household supplies, and non-alcoholic mixer kits will be delivered and stocked prior to Owner's arrival.
5. Insurance, Care & Limitation of Liability: Craft Clean Services, LLC d/b/a Panhandle Provision maintains active Commercial General Liability insurance covering its in-home delivery and stocking operations. Provider shall exercise reasonable care while inside the residence. Owner agrees to release, indemnify, and hold harmless Craft Clean Services, LLC d/b/a Panhandle Provision from any claims, losses, or damages resulting from pre-existing property conditions, faulty locks or security systems, refrigerator or appliance malfunctions, power outages, or inaccurate access credentials provided by Owner.
6. Effective Period & Revocation: This authorization applies to the arrival date specified below and any subsequent orders placed by Owner with Provider, unless revoked by Owner in writing prior to order dispatch.
