Master Vendor & Property Access Terms

1. Approved Vendor Status & Blanket Turnover Access: The Property Management Company identified below ("Manager") hereby approves Craft Clean Services, LLC d/b/a Panhandle Provision ("Provider"), including its vetted personnel, as an authorized concierge delivery vendor for vacation rental properties managed by Manager. Manager grants Provider express permission to enter any managed unit for which an incoming guest has placed a confirmed provisioning order, strictly during Manager's designated turnover window (typically 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM on check-in days, unless otherwise directed by Manager), solely to deliver, unpack, and stock groceries and household provisions inside the kitchen, refrigerator, freezer, and pantry prior to guest arrival.
2. Representation of Management Authority: Manager warrants and represents that its property management agreements with the respective property owners authorize Manager to grant third-party service and concierge vendors temporary access to managed properties for guest-requested services.
3. Vendor Code Security & Photo Verification: Provider agrees to hold all vendor keypad codes, lockbox combinations, smart-lock PINs, and gate instructions provided by Manager in strict confidence and use them exclusively for scheduled guest deliveries. Upon completing in-home stocking, Provider shall capture time-stamped verification photographs of the stocked kitchen/refrigerator (available to Manager upon request) and verify that all exterior doors and entry points are securely locked upon exit.
4. Strict Compliance with Florida Alcohol Laws: In accordance with Florida law, Provider never delivers, places, or leaves alcoholic beverages unattended inside an unoccupied rental property. Pre-arrival deliveries consist exclusively of non-alcoholic groceries, beverages, household essentials, and non-alcoholic craft mixer kits.
5. Insurance & Mutual Indemnification: Provider maintains active Commercial General Liability insurance covering its in-home delivery and stocking operations ("care, custody, and control") and shall furnish a Certificate of Insurance (COI) naming Manager as Certificate Holder prior to accessing properties. Provider shall exercise reasonable care inside every residence and indemnify Manager for property damage directly caused by Provider's negligence. Manager releases and holds Provider harmless from claims arising out of pre-existing property conditions, faulty locks/alarms, appliance or power failures, or inaccurate access codes supplied by Manager.
6. Guest Welcome Email Integration & Term: Manager authorizes Provider to supply a dedicated booking link (www.panhandleprovision.com) for optional inclusion in Manager's pre-arrival guest communications at zero cost to Manager. This Master Agreement remains in effect across Manager's portfolio until terminated by either party upon written notice.
